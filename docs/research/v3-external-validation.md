# v3 possession pipeline and its external validation

Status: implemented and validated on public external data; run locally on the match on 2026-10-25
(PLAN E2), where it over-called stoppages. Superseded as the default by v4
([v4-robust-dead-ball.md](v4-robust-dead-ball.md)); still reproducible with
`--model config/possession_v3.json`. All numbers below are aggregates on Metrica's public sample
games, never on the match.

## Method (`possession/`, entry point `run.py`)

1. **Read the tactical ATD.**
   - The reader is driven by the EPTS `DataFormatSpecification`, with the baseline's slot order as
     the fallback.
   - Row = ATD frame_id − 1, which is the video frame.
2. **Per-frame features, tracking only.**
   - *Team, antisymmetric* (swapping teams negates them):
     - nearest home vs. away distance and control advantage;
     - control episodes (≥ 5 frames within 1.2 m, uncontested);
     - dribbles (player moving with the ball);
     - receptions and kicks (sharp ball-speed change next to a player).
   - *Team shape, ball-free*: block position and velocity towards the attacking direction, and
     width/length differences, each over 2 s and 6 s.
   - *Dead ball*:
     - collective player speed over 1, 3 and 10 s, and the share of slow players;
     - ball visibility, out of bounds, stillness;
     - current gap length, and whether the gap starts or ends near a line;
     - distance to the centre spot.
3. **Two logistic regressions** fitted on external games only:
   - P(dead), with an intercept;
   - P(home | alive), with no intercept, so no home/away prior can be learned.
   - The coefficients and switching costs live in `config/possession_v3.json` (≈ 4 KB). They are
     regenerated byte for byte by `scripts/train_model.py`.
4. **Two-stage exact Viterbi**, offline and non-causal.
   - Stage 1 decodes alive vs. dead per half (cost 40).
   - Stage 2 decodes H vs. A inside each alive run (cost 8).
   - Boundaries fall where the evidence turns, with no window lag, and a stoppage is never a cheap
     shortcut between H and A.
5. **Pre-kickoff.** Frames before each half's Smart Tagging `Kick Off (Start)` window are D. The
   second half is graded from 110,808 but kicks off later (FAQ.md). No frame constants are used.
6. **Output.** H/A/D on graded frames and X outside them. The official `validate_submission.py`
   runs on the exact file, and a manifest of aggregates goes to `MTAH_WORK_DIR`.

## External benchmark

- **Data:** Metrica's public sample games 1–3 (<https://github.com/metrica-sports/sample-data>).
  These are other games, which RULES.md allows.
- **Labels:** H/A/D per frame derived from Metrica's event data (`possession/external.py`).
  - Possession changes when the other team starts an on-ball action.
  - Loose balls stay with the last team.
  - The ball is dead from BALL OUT, a foul or a stoppage until the next restart.
- **Degradation:** the clean sample tracking is made to look like the ATD.
  - The ball is missing on ~41 % of graded frames, in long bursts and mostly during stoppages.
  - A stationary ball is placed before restarts, there are occasional false detections, and
    players get noise, dropouts and same-team identity swaps.
- **Calibration:** on this benchmark the official baseline scores close to its published
  full-match values, and in the same order.
  - smooth50: 0.247 here vs. 0.2433 published.
  - smooth125: 0.222 vs. 0.2242.
- **Protocol:** leave one game out.
  - Fit and choose costs on two games (noise seeds 0–1); score the third (seeds 10–12) with the
    official `possession_metrics.evaluate`.
  - Paired bootstrap over GT possessions, pooled over 3 games × 3 seeds.

| id | change | spellF1 | macroF1 | possessions (gt 381) | Δ spellF1 vs b50 [5 %, 95 %] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| b125 | official baseline, ±125 | 0.222 | 0.595 | 362 | −0.024 [−0.033, −0.016] |
| b50 | official baseline, ±50 (published floor) | 0.247 | 0.633 | 544 | reference |
| v1 | Viterbi + learned dead ball + distance evidence | 0.484 | 0.828 | 241 | +0.238 [+0.226, +0.251] |
| v2_all | v1 + touch features + team shape (2 s) | 0.531 | 0.851 | 250 | — |
| **v3** | v2_all + team shape (6 s), shipped | **0.544** | **0.855** | 253 | **+0.298 [+0.286, +0.312]** |

Step gains, paired:
- touch features: +0.020 [+0.014, +0.026] over v1;
- shape (2 s): +0.037 [+0.029, +0.045] over v1;
- shape (6 s): +0.013 [+0.007, +0.018] over v2_all.

**Robustness.** Training uses the default noise; test noise varies.

| test noise | v3 | b50 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| default | 0.544 | 0.254 | +0.29 |
| more ball missing in play | 0.520 | 0.214 | +0.31 |
| ball often visible in stoppages | 0.519 | 0.227 | +0.29 |
| longer gaps (median 15 s) | 0.546 | 0.259 | +0.29 |
| much noisier positions | 0.369 | 0.199 | +0.17 |

**Where the remaining error is** (oracle swaps on held-out games):
- perfect team evidence → ~0.83;
- perfect dead ball → ~0.57.

Team evidence is the bottleneck. v3 also predicts ~250 possessions against 381, so short
possessions are still missed.

## Limits

- The external labels come from events, while the match GT comes from corrected-tracking
  ownership; conventions may differ. The noise model is an approximation. Absolute scores will not
  transfer; the gain over the baseline is the claim.
- Not yet measured on the match. The public 5 minutes are a sanity check only, never a selection
  criterion (upstream RULES.md, 23 Oct 2026).

## Reproduce

```sh
git clone https://github.com/metrica-sports/sample-data ~/mtah-external/sample-data
python scripts/train_model.py ~/mtah-external/sample-data/data            # -> config/possession_v3.json
python scripts/validate_external.py ~/mtah-external/sample-data/data --variants v3,v1 --ref b50
python scripts/validate_external.py ~/mtah-external/sample-data/data --noise longer_gaps
python -m unittest discover -s possession -p 'test_*.py'
```

`scripts/make_fixture.py` writes a full-size bundle in ATD format plus its own GT, built from one
public sample game. It rehearses the whole local workflow (run, validate, score) without match data.
For an out-of-sample comparison with another pipeline, retrain with `MTAH_EXCLUDE_GAME=<game>`.

## Related work

- Vidal-Codina et al. 2022 (arXiv:2202.00804), possession and duel zones; implemented in databallpy.
- Link & Hoernig 2017 (PLoS ONE), individual possession.
- Lang et al. 2022 (Sci. Rep.), ball in play from players only.
- PathCRF (Kim et al. 2026) and Ball Radar (Kim et al. 2023), possession without the ball.
- Public repositories of other teams in this challenge converge on the same family: Viterbi with
  switching costs, dead ball kept separate from "ball not seen", and team shape in ball gaps.
  Ideas only; no code reused.
